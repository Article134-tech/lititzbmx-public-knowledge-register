## Third-party historical material

Historical catalogs, advertisements, flyers, magazine pages, photographs, scans, trademarks, and other source materials remain subject to the rights of their respective creators, publishers, brands, archives, and owners.

This repository does not reproduce protected historical source scans. It preserves metadata, citations, public URLs, paraphrased bibliographic facts, evidence notes, limitations, and research status.

A source citation or public URL documents evidence and provenance. It does not imply that Lititz BMX owns, endorses, or has permission to redistribute the underlying third-party work.
