BRAND-JMC

JMC Racing

Business origin, BMX entry, prototypes and production are documented as separate phases.

JMC Racing's public history is strongest when its phases remain separate: a bicycle business established in 1969, direct BMX involvement around 1974, a shop team in 1975, XP prototype development in 1976 and production-frame launches in March and April 1977. Model and serial conclusions remain tied to period, construction and company records.

What the Evidence Supports

Rejected claimDate

JMC began producing BMX frames in 1969.

Approved public wording: The business that became JMC was established in 1969; BMX involvement developed in the mid-1970s and production frames began in 1977.

Why this status: JMC's history separates business origin, BMX involvement, prototype development and production-frame launch.

What this does not prove: The 1969 business date is not a BMX frame-production date.

Sources: About JMC, How to ID a JMC

Last reviewed 2026-07-30 · Permalink to this claim

AttributedIdentity

JMC1 and JMC20 were the first production short and long frames.

Approved public wording: JMC's archive identifies JMC1 and JMC20 as its first production short and long frames, dated March and April 1977.

Why this status: The company identification guide preserves the serials, dates and short/long roles.

What this does not prove: Any present-day claimant frame still requires serial, construction, provenance and certification evidence.

Sources: How to ID a JMC

Last reviewed 2026-07-30 · Permalink to this claim

Rejected claimSpecification

The first two digits of every JMC serial identify the production year.

Approved public wording: JMC serial interpretation depends on model, period, prefix and construction features; the first two digits are not a universal year code.

Why this status: Early frames used sequential JMC numbers, model prefixes appeared later and the public archive describes a transition rather than a universal rule.

Sources: How to ID a JMC
