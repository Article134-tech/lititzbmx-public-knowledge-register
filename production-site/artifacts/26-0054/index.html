26.0054

Felicia Stancil's 2012 Worlds GT

Achievement verified; exact surviving bicycle remains under authentication.

Lititz BMX artifact 26.0054 is strongly attributed as Felicia Stancil's 2012 Worlds GT Speed Series bicycle with a W1 plate. Official reporting verifies her Junior Women time-trial and race world titles in Birmingham. The exact surviving frame, plate and race-day component configuration remain under authentication.

What the Evidence Supports

VerifiedGrade AResult

Felicia Stancil won both the Junior Women time-trial and race world titles in Birmingham in 2012.

Approved public wording: Official USA BMX reporting verifies Felicia Stancil's Junior Women time-trial and race world titles at the 2012 UCI BMX World Championships in Birmingham.

Why this status: USA BMX identifies Stancil as the Factory GT rider who won the time-trial superfinal and the Junior Women race title.

What this does not prove: The result does not by itself authenticate the surviving Lititz BMX frame, W1 plate or current component configuration.

Sources: UCI Worlds Update

Last reviewed 2026-07-30 · Permalink to this claim

Qualified conclusionGrade B-Identity

Artifact 26.0054 is the exact bicycle Stancil used for the 2012 world-title performances.

Approved public wording: Artifact 26.0054 is strongly attributed as Stancil's 2012 Worlds GT Speed Series bicycle, but exact frame and race-day configuration authentication remains open.

Why this status: The Lititz inventory, W1 plate and collection provenance create a strong attribution; serial, plate-reverse, transfer and race-day visual evidence are still required.

What this does not prove: The present attribution does not establish that every current component was fitted during both championship rides.

Sources: Lititz BMX Artifact 26.0054, UCI Worlds Update

What Remains Open

Why it matters: They may tie the surviving frame to a production period and comparison examples.
