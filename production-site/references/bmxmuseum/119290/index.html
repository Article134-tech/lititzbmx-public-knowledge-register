BMXMuseum 119290 — Submitted as 1984 JMC Darrell Young

Exact submitted metadata is public; individual-frame originality and configuration remain unverified.

BMXMuseum record 119290 is an exact public record submitted as a 1984 JMC Racing Darrell Young. JMC’s first-party chronology and a March 1984 period test support the model’s historical existence and period. The submitted bicycle’s serial, provenance, restoration history and factory-correct configuration have not been inspected by Lititz BMX.

What the Evidence Supports

VerifiedIdentity

BMXMuseum record 119290 is publicly submitted as a 1984 JMC Racing Darrell Young.

Approved public wording: The exact public record ID, submitted year and submitted model title are recovered.

Why this status: A first-party public social-discovery route exposed the canonical BMXMuseum record and submitted metadata.

What this does not prove: Submitted metadata does not authenticate the serial, provenance, paint, components or originality.

VerifiedContext

A JMC Darrell Young Design complete bicycle existed and was tested in March 1984.

Approved public wording: A March 1984 period test independently documents a JMC Darrell Young Design complete bicycle and one measured build configuration.

Why this status: The test records the model, measured geometry, weight and the components fitted to the tested bicycle.

What this does not prove: One magazine test build is not a universal factory specification for every Darrell Young bicycle.

Sources: JMC Darrell Young Design — Bicycles and Dirt, March 1984, JMC Racing Historical Archive
