Sources, Rights & Corrections

A public claim is not complete until readers can inspect the source identity, evidence role, limitation, rights treatment and correction path supporting it.

Claim visibility

A badge is never the evidence.

Every claim card must show the proposition, determination, approved public wording, plain-language reasoning, source links and any boundary needed to prevent overreading.

What this does not prove

Submitted records, rarity claims and official achievements tied to surviving artifacts require an explicit boundary. The public interface states what a source supports and what it cannot establish.

Source classes

Sources are not displayed as interchangeable.

The evidence role tells readers why the source is useful and where its authority ends.

Official

Sanctioning body, manufacturer or institutional source used for the exact fact it publishes.

First-person

Founder, rider, builder, family or participant account—valuable and visibly attributed rather than treated as independent.

Period publication

Contemporary magazine, advertisement, result, program or price object.

Period catalog

Year- and market-specific manufacturer or distributor documentation.

Current first-party

A present-day official history or product page; retrospective and modern facts remain separated.

Independent

Catalog reconstruction, business history, technical analysis or secondary source with its own limitations.

Submitted metadata

External collection or bike submission used for discovery and comparison, not factory authentication.

Public backlink

An ordinary public link exposing an exact external ID, title or research lead.

Physical evidence

Lititz-owned object observations, serials, labels, dimensions, condition and provenance records.

Rights and reuse

Publicly accessible does not mean public domain.

The register can index, cite and summarize a source without acquiring the right to mirror it.

Link only

Readers are directed to the source; content is not copied.

Index only

Bibliographic identity and limited factual fields are recorded; scans or expressive content are not republished.

Factual paraphrase

Original Lititz BMX wording summarizes limited facts with attribution and visible limitations.

Lititz-owned

Original Lititz BMX research, photographs or documentation may be published according to the record-specific notice.

Permission required

The source is a useful lead but copying or public preservation requires a separate rights basis.

Corrections

Material changes remain visible.

Corrections preserve the prior wording, the corrected wording, the reason, the evidence and the review date.

Identify the record

Use the stable Record ID or Claim ID, not only a page title.

Identify the issue

Quote or describe the disputed, incomplete or misleading statement.

Supply evidence

Provide a source, physical observation or first-person correction that can be inspected.

Preserve the change

Material corrections remain in the revision history rather than silently disappearing.
