1981 Hutch Pro Racer — $419.75

An official retrospective historical price, not a recovered period price-sheet transcription.

Hutch’s current official company history reports a 1981 retail price of $419.75 for the Pro Racer. The register preserves the value as an official retrospective historical price because the original 1981 catalog, advertisement or manufacturer price sheet has not yet been recovered. It is not presented as a universal dealer transaction price.
