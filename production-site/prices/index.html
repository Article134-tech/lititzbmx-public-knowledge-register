Historical Prices

Manufacturer MSRP, distributor lists, retailer advertisements, catalog fees and current retail are different evidence types. No inflation adjustment, silent averaging or marketplace valuation is performed.

Price rules

A number without its basis is not a useful historical price.

Every observation retains its source date, currency, geography or market context, product type and limitation.

Manufacturer or official history

Strongest when a period price sheet exists; current retrospective values remain labeled as retrospective.

Distributor list

Market, currency, unit and dealer context must stay attached to the value.

Retailer advertisement

Seller-specific advertised price—not manufacturer MSRP or a universal transaction price.

Catalog or sticker-pack fee

Distribution/promotion cost, deliberately separated from bicycles and components.
